Version: 1.0.0
  Info:
    - Renamed to AccumulatorFarm+ (internal name AccumulatorFarmPlus), now a standalone extension. Versioning restarts at 1.0.0.
    - Based on AccumulatorFarm (MIT) by EliteRamsay - https://mods.factorio.com/mod/AccumulatorFarm
    - Item, entity, recipe and technology names of the three farms are unchanged, so placed farms keep working.
  Features:
    - With Space Age, the original technologies small/medium/large-accumulator-farm (solar-energy) are removed
    - With Space Age, new technologies accumulator-farm-plus-tier-1/2/3 unlock the three farms after the Electromagnetic Plant
    - Tier 1 requires Electromagnetic Plant: 1000x (automation, logistic, chemical, space, electromagnetic), 60s
    - Tier 2 requires Tier 1: 2500x (automation, logistic, chemical, space, 2x electromagnetic), 60s
    - Tier 3 requires Tier 2: 5000x (automation, logistic, chemical, space, 3x electromagnetic), 60s
    - Without Space Age the original three technologies and recipes stay as before

Version: 0.1.0
  Features:
    - Optional Space Age support ("? space-age" dependency). Without Space Age nothing changes.
    - With Space Age, all three farms are crafted in the Electromagnetic Plant (recipe category "electromagnetics")
    - With Space Age, small farm: 10x accumulator + 20x superconductor; buffer 10GJ, input/output 1GW
    - With Space Age, medium farm: 2x small farm + 20x supercapacitor; buffer 100GJ, input/output 10GW
    - With Space Age, large farm: 2x medium farm + 40x superconductor + 40x supercapacitor + 100x electrolyte; buffer 1TJ, input/output 100GW
  Locale:
    - Added item descriptions for the Space Age tiers
